03   Range & charging

Go further. Charge smarter.

Designed around the realities of everyday riding, with the range and charging experience to make electric performance practical.

How far can I ride?
ORRA ONE is specified for a range of up to 180 km on a full charge. As with any electric vehicle, real-world range depends on speed, temperature, terrain and riding mode.
How does charging work?
You can charge at home or at public fast-charging stations. Your ORRA specialist will walk you through charging times and the right home set-up for how you ride.
Where can I charge?
At home, at work, and on the public charging networks across Germany, France, Belgium and the Netherlands. We help you plan around the stations on the routes you ride most.
Is it suitable for longer journeys?
Yes, with a little planning. Longer rides work best with a fast-charging stop built into the route, usually the same moment you would stop for a coffee anyway.
