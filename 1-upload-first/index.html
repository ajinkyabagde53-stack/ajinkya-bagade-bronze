AI agent · in development

Overseer

The idea: most content plans start from a hunch. Overseer starts from evidence. Give it a topic and it studies what already works on YouTube, finds the gaps nobody is covering, ranks the ideas worth making, and drafts the brief and script. Then it reads the results and gets sharper with every round.
